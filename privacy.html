Privacy Policy — Agent Deck

Cumulative Web Inc  ·  Effective: September 19, 2026  ·  Contact: hp@cumulativeweb.com

Agent Deck is a connector built by Cumulative Web Inc ("CWI", "we") that lets Meta Muse users search and use CWI's music catalog, catalog metadata, and music-industry tools. This policy explains what happens to information when you use it.

1. What the connector does with your requests

When you use Agent Deck through Muse, the text of your request (for example, a search query like "find a 140 BPM trap track") is transmitted to CWI's public catalog services so the connector can return an answer. We use that request only to produce the response — to search the catalog, look up metadata such as ISRCs, or run the requested tool.

2. What we do not collect

  • Agent Deck does not require a CWI account, and we do not collect your name, email address, location, or payment information through the connector.
  • We do not build profiles of users, sell data, or share request contents with advertisers or data brokers.
  • The connector's catalog endpoints are public and read-only. We do not accept payments through the connector.

3. Logs

Like most web services, our servers keep short-lived operational logs (timestamps, requested endpoints, error codes) for security and reliability. These logs are not linked to your identity and are retained for no more than 90 days, then deleted.

4. What Meta and Muse do with your data

Your conversation with Muse, including anything you send to the Agent Deck connector, is also governed by Meta's own privacy policy. We do not control how Meta stores or processes your Muse conversations. Please review Meta's policy for details.

5. Cookies and tracking

The connector itself sets no cookies and runs no third-party trackers. Our company website may use standard, minimal analytics; see that site's own notices.

6. Children

Agent Deck is a general-audience business tool and is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children.

7. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy in a material way, we will update the effective date above and publish the new version at this URL before the change takes effect.
